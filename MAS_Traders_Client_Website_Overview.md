# M.A.S. Traders Website Overview

## Project Summary

The M.A.S. Traders website is a **premium industrial catalogue and quotation-request platform** built for customers in Kovilpatti and beyond. It combines a clean, technical visual direction with a practical B2B purchasing journey: customers can discover products, build a requirement list, request a quotation, and continue the conversation through WhatsApp.

The site has been designed to present M.A.S. Traders as a clear, dependable industrial supplier without adding unsupported product claims. The approved M.A.S. Traders logo has been retained exactly as provided, and the experience is responsive across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

> **Client objective:** Make industrial product discovery faster, reduce ambiguity in customer requirements, and create a straightforward path from catalogue browsing to a quotation conversation.

## Customer Journey

The website is structured around a simple industrial buying flow. A customer first lands on the homepage, where the main product range and catalogue entry points are immediately visible. They can then use the Product Finder, browse categories, or open a specific product page.

If the customer knows the required items, they can add them to **My Requirement** and set quantities. If the customer does not yet know the exact product, they can still open **Request a Quote** and submit a general enquiry with contact details and optional notes or reference files. This keeps the RFQ process useful for both prepared buyers and customers who need guidance.

Once the RFQ is submitted, the system saves the request, provides a reference number, and opens WhatsApp with the quote summary prefilled for the M.A.S. Traders business number. If the customer selected files, the website clearly instructs them to attach those files manually in WhatsApp before sending. Call and email alternatives remain visible for customers who prefer another contact method.

The website is deployed and ready for client review. The product catalogue, responsive product discovery, general and product-based RFQ flows, official PDF download, WhatsApp quote handoff, and mobile usability improvements are in place.
