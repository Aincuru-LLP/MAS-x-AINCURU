# Chosen Direction — Signal & Structure

## Design Movement

Contemporary **Swiss industrial editorial design** translated into a B2B catalogue: typographic hierarchy, disciplined alignment, functional negative space, and restrained technical notation.

## Core Principles

1. **Utility leads:** Each visual element must make product discovery or the quote workflow clearer.
2. **Precision over decoration:** Thin rules, index numbers, calibrated spacing, and controlled blue accents create industrial character without visual noise.
3. **Product-forward framing:** Large, clean product studies sit within consistent neutral frames; categories are navigational devices, not promotional tiles.
4. **Calm confidence:** Editorial whitespace and rigorous typography replace exaggerated claims, generic photography, and crowded UI.

## Color Philosophy

The experience is predominantly refined off-white, creating a clean working surface for catalogue reading. The approved M.A.S. blue carries interaction, selection, and directional cues, while deep navy delivers typographic weight and technical contrast. Blue is used like an engineer’s mark—deliberate and sparing, never as wallpaper.

## Layout Paradigm

The page follows a **technical index** structure rather than a centered landing-page stack. A narrow left information rail carries section labels, numerical markers, and context; the primary content field uses asymmetrical image/text compositions. On mobile, the rail converts to horizontal index labels and the content becomes a purposeful sequence.

## Signature Elements

1. A blue vertical index line with short technical markers that anchors major sections.
2. Fine coordinate grids, kept low-contrast and used only to organize hero and category imagery.
3. Blue underlines and crop brackets that appear on hover to indicate selectable catalogue objects.

## Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should confirm intent, not entertain. Search responds immediately, product cards lift subtly within their image frame, and the requirement builder remains visibly available as a practical working list. The primary quote route is always direct and never framed as a shopping cart.

## Animation

Use transform- and opacity-based reveals only. The logo and hero text enter in a staggered editorial sequence; product media reveals through a short clipped transform; hover states shift an accent line and image by a few pixels. Motion is disabled or simplified for reduced-motion preferences.

## Typography System

Use **Space Grotesk** for editorial headings and technical labels, paired with **Manrope** for highly legible body copy and UI controls. Headings use tight tracking and clear size steps; small uppercase labels use generous tracking; body copy remains sentence case and compact.

## Brand Essence

**M.A.S. Traders is a practical industrial supply partner for teams that need to find the right materials and turn requirements into clear quote requests.**

**Personality:** precise, dependable, composed.

## Brand Voice

Headlines are concise and workmanlike; CTAs name the practical next action. Microcopy explains process without inflated claims.

> "Find the right material for the job."

> "Build your requirement. Request a quote when ready."

## Wordmark & Logo

The supplied M.A.S. Traders logo remains the sole approved mark. It is displayed as provided, without redraw, effect, proportional change, or alternate typography.
